Privacy Policy

Updated October 22, 2026

Who we are

Guitar Blast is provided by Hakkı İlhan Altan. For privacy questions or requests, contact guitarblastapp@gmail.com. This policy describes data used by the app and its integrated services.

Progress and preferences

The app stores game progress, stars, learned notes, practice settings and preferences on your device to operate the game. Device or iCloud backups may include this local app data according to your Apple settings. Local data remains until it is reset or removed. Deleting the app does not cancel subscriptions or delete information held separately by Apple or Google.

Microphone and device permissions

With your permission, guitar modes and the tuner analyze microphone input on your device to detect musical pitch. The app does not save or upload microphone recordings. You can revoke microphone permission in iOS Settings; tapping modes remain available. Optional notifications and image-saving features use the permissions you grant in iOS.

Analytics, diagnostics and Game Center

The app integrates Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. Usage and purchase events, session information, app-generated and app-instance identifiers, device and app metadata, and crash diagnostics can be processed to understand usage and improve reliability. An app-generated identifier links events from the same installation. Providers may process data outside your country and retain it according to their service settings and policies. Apple Game Center processes your game profile, scores and achievements when you use it.

See the providers’ policies for more information: Firebase, Google, and Apple.

Purchases and payments

Apple processes payments. The app uses product identifiers, verified transaction information and expiration status to provide and restore paid access. The app does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple controls App Store account and payment records under its privacy policy.

Your choices and requests

You can change device permissions, notifications, tracking authorization and Game Center settings in iOS. Tracking permission can be denied without preventing core gameplay; denying tracking does not turn off all app analytics.

To request access, correction or deletion of data associated with you, or ask about retention, contact guitarblastapp@gmail.com. Provide enough information to locate the relevant record without sending payment-card details. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and necessary verification. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Children

We do not ask children to provide names or contact details in the game. The integrated services described above may still process technical usage data. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, contact guitarblastapp@gmail.com.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The updated version and date appear here and in the app. Send privacy or support questions to guitarblastapp@gmail.com.
