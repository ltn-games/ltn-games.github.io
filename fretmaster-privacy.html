FretMaster – Guitar Note Trainer Privacy Policy

Welcome to FretMaster – Guitar Note Trainer (the "App"), provided by LTN Games ("we," "us," or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting information collected through your use of the App. This Privacy Policy describes what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in the following ways:

  • Usage data: When you access the App we automatically collect data such as your IP address, device type, operating system, unique device identifiers (including the Apple Identifier for Advertisers), gameplay activity, session duration, achievements, practice progress, and in-app purchase history. This information is generally processed in an aggregated or de-identified form.
  • Data from third-party services: We rely on trusted partners to deliver core App functionality. These services collect and process data in accordance with their own policies:
    • Advertising: We use advertising partners to display ads. Their SDKs may collect device identifiers, coarse location data, and usage metrics to deliver, measure, and personalize ads. Review their privacy practices here: Google AdMob, Unity Ads.
    • In-app purchases: All purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card or billing details. Apple may share anonymized confirmation data with us so we can unlock purchased content. You can read Apple’s privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.
    • Analytics: To understand engagement and improve the App, we may use analytics services such as Google Analytics for Firebase. These tools collect aggregated usage insights and crash diagnostics.

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate, maintain, and provide core App features including note identification, scale practice, and chord drills.
  • Track your practice progress, achievements, and mastery levels to personalize your learning experience.
  • Support in-app purchases, rewards, and player progression.
  • Display relevant advertising and measure campaign performance.
  • Analyze usage trends to enhance gameplay, stability, and accessibility.
  • Respond to support requests, bug reports, and player feedback.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only as necessary to support the App:

  • Service providers: Advertising, analytics, customer support, and crash reporting partners.
  • Legal compliance: When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to defend our rights and users.
  • Business transfers: If we engage in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as permitted by law.

4. Your Choices

  • Limit personalized ads by enabling the "Limit Ad Tracking" (iOS) or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (Android) settings.
  • Reset your advertising identifier in your device settings to reduce personalized targeting.
  • Review the privacy settings provided by third-party services and SDKs you interact with while using the App.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 years old (or 16 years old where required by local law, including the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will promptly delete the information.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. When changes are material, we will take reasonable steps to notify you, such as highlighting the change in the App listing or within the App.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the App, please contact us at support@ltngames.com.
